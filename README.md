# A Causal Local Recording Framework for Deterministic Pattern Formation: Emergence of Geometric Symmetry on Water Surfaces

This repository contains the simulation codes used in the manuscript:

"Deterministic Origin of Symmetry in Confined Water Surfaces: A Structural Approach Based on Causal Recording of Local Rules "


## Requirements
- Python 3.x
- NumPy
- Matplotlib

## Figure Correspondence

- `Figure_2B_surface_record_dual_source.py`  
  Simulation of surface records generated by dual localized sources.

- `Figure_3A and Figure_3B_simulated_water_surface (side and center).py`  
  Comparison between simulated surface records and experimental water surface pattern.

- `Figure_4_1_symmetrical_geometric_order_single_source.py`  
  Emergence of symmetrical geometric order through constrained causal interactions.

- `Figure_4_2_symmetrical_geometric_order_array.py`  
  Emergence of symmetrical geometric order through constrained causal interactions.

- `Figure_S1AB_2A_surface_record_single_source.py`  
  Single localized source surface records.

- `Figure_S2_Validation of spatial propagation arrest via the beta_field.py`  
  Surface records generated from multiple localized events with randomized initiation times and positions.

- `Figure_S3_Simulated_water_surface_center.py
  The emergent surface patterns under varying local time steps

- `Figure_S4_symmetrical_geometric_order_randmonized_event.py
  Robustness of temporal symmetry under stochastic spatial and temporal inputs


## Notes
The scripts are provided to support transparency and reproducibility of the proposed formulation. 
No additional interaction rules beyond the governing framework are imposed.
